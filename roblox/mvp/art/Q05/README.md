# Q05 figure art proposal

This is a reviewable native-geometry proposal, not approved faithful production art. Each figure is a small rounded capsule character: one cream or sage body, one contrasting belly panel, two ink eyes, and a distinct silhouette accent. The vending display uses seven labeled slots; final figure identities and reference fidelity require independent art review.

These swatches are a concrete review option, not a claim that they match official Big into Energy reference colors. Before production approval, compare against approved references, capture exact source colors, and decide whether faithful figure colors may extend beyond the world palette.
