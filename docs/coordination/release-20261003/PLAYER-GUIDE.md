# Phuong's birthday game

This describes the integrated October 3 candidate. Publication and independent acceptance are recorded separately in PUBLICATION.md and the verifier report.

## The goal and route

Celebrate what makes Phuong, Phuong. Friends help make her room cozy, spend time with Jasper, make a miniature, read under the blossoms and assemble a meal. Those five activities each contribute one petal and 20 to the shared birthday fund. The group uses the fund to fulfill Phuong's Labubu wish. Her wish is the sixth petal and opens the birthday celebration.

1. Enter from the birthday lobby into Phuong's bedroom. The goal display and destination marker show the next stop. On a small landscape phone, tap Goals to expand the journal and other controls.
2. Make the bed: pick an item, place it, and follow the remaining-item instructions. Confirm the final style. Shelf tidying is an optional extra with its own object and slot choices.
3. Greet Jasper in the bedroom, follow him to the yard, find his toy, play gentle fetch and give him a treat.
4. Make a miniature at the UDistrict craft booth. Choose a part, turn it if desired and place it. The miniature also appears in the bedroom; its finishing choice is cosmetic.
5. Read in the Quad. Choose a highlight, reaction and bookmark, then save and finish. Typing a personal note is optional and private to its author.
6. Build the meal at FOB, add the sauce at Aladdin's and finish the table setting at the serving table. Each stage identifies the next location.
7. Visit the Labubu machine. Phuong chooses and confirms her wish; friends help with the shared fund. A box costs 20, and the selected figure is guaranteed within five boxes. The five earning activities provide enough for that guarantee. The collection and latest result remain visible if a reveal is closed.
8. When all six petals are complete, follow the birthday invitation to the patio.

There is no need to grind replays to afford the first wish. Optional replays are available after first completion. Progress belongs to the running server session; it is not a permanent saved collection across separate servers.

## The birthday moment

Friends gather around the cake table. Once the invitation is ready, Phuong sitting in her designated seat starts the birthday moment once. Guests sitting first do not trigger it.

The celebration offers a short, optional birthday camera pan with birthday text and the selected birthday audio. Each person can watch or skip without changing the group's progress. When the moment finishes, Phuong cuts the cake, everyone takes a personal slice and uses Enjoy cake to eat. The activity panel closes so the eating action can be seen. Afterwards the goal becomes photos, friends and free exploration.

The implementation does not synthesize the participants' voices. Actual people can sing together; the game supplies its selected birthday track. Music loading in Studio is not a claim that cloud audio permissions or a listening check passed.

## Walkthrough impression and evidence limits

The clearest payoffs in the coordinator's actual walkthrough were opening the wanted Labubu and finishing together at the cake table. The route offers variety, although some actions still rely on menus rather than rich character animation. Initial phone friction came from crowded panels, buried choices and unclear next steps; those findings produced the final compact goal display, direct actions and automatic next-step scrolling.

The ordinary route was completed with one real Andrew Studio client. A separate phone-emulated run exercised touch controls and a clearly labeled Play-only Phuong-role fixture. That is useful functional evidence, not a real family group's opinion or a physical-phone performance test.
