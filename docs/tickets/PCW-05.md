# PCW-05 — Recreate the UDistrict fair block

**Status:** Planned; not started  
**Priority:** P2 — after foundation  
**Suggested owner:** Environment art (unassigned)  
**Dependencies:** 01; 03  
**Owned scope:** Future street facades, tents, crossing and props

## Purpose

Bring the reference street's recognizable rhythm into the compact world.

## Deliverable

104 × 158 fair block, eight tents, masonry corners, awnings, curved lamps, banner, crosswalks, craft/display sockets.

## Acceptance criteria

- 24-stud center lane and 20-stud crossing stay unobstructed.
- North and east exits connect.
- No old event date.
- Reference morphology visible from above and player height.

## Limits and open decisions

No shop economy, crowd simulation, extra interiors or invented must-have businesses. Storefront naming remains a content choice.

## Validation

Compare against aerial and close-up reference images and later check touch-camera sightlines.

## Execution boundary

Ticket creation is authorized. Implementation, scripting, Studio building and publishing are not started in this task. The user explicitly requested no code. Follow the [approved map](../design/map-proposal-v0.2.md) and [visual brief](../design/visual-direction-v0.1.md); measurements remain test targets.
