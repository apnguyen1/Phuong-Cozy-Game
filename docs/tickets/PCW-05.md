# PCW-05 — Three named UDistrict storefronts

**Status:** Implementation authorized; Draft 02 under independent review. Human acceptance is pending.

**September 26, 2026 scope amendment:** The active September 26 request authorizes three named stores and more complete decoration. The user confirmed FOB Poke Bar. This replaces the old eight-tent fair-block deliverable, provisional dimensions, unnamed-business/no-interiors constraints and no-code boundary for this ticket. Historical text is preserved below.

## Purpose

Turn the western street's temporary shop masses into a recognizable, warm UDistrict-inspired block.

## Deliverable

Three finished storefronts ordered north to south: FOB Poke Bar, HeyTea and Aladdins. Include readable signage, windows/awnings, furnishings, food/tea identity and accessible interiors. Aladdins refers to the Ave restaurant; the architecture may remain stylized.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exterior names read FOB, HeyTea and Aladdins; FOB is clearly identified as FOB Poke Bar by its secondary sign and food props.
- Each store has a finished facade, framed windows/door, awning or comparable detail, and furnished interior appropriate to its identity.
- All three shops can be entered from the sidewalk and exited without collision traps or inaccessible thresholds.
- The 24-stud street, pedestrian connections and continuous sidewalk remain unobstructed after Quad integration.
- North-to-south order remains FOB, HeyTea, Aladdins.
- No obsolete event date, invented personal message, shop economy or unrequested quest is added.
- Approved palette roles and warm stylized readability survive at player height.

## Limits and open decisions

Preserve the current plots and user's whole-world placement. Exact real-world facade replicas, branded logos, purchasing, NPC shopkeepers and food gameplay are outside this revision. Human acceptance is pending.

## Validation

Inspect all three street signs and interiors; walk each doorway/counter, sidewalk and crossing. Check seating orientation, flicker, collision and identity/provenance notes. MAP-02 provides the integrated packet.
