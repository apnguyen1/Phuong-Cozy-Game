# PCW-06 — Reference-based blossom Quad

**Status:** Implementation authorized; Draft 02 under independent review. Human acceptance is pending.

**September 26, 2026 scope amendment:** The supplied September 26 overhead photograph governs the path pattern. This supersedes the old provisional footprint, fixed eight-tree/two-bench counts and no-code boundary. Existing destinations remain connected across the expanded shared landscape. Historical text is preserved below.

## Purpose

Replace the Draft 01 path pattern with a recognizable UW Cherry Blossom Quad-inspired arrangement.

## Deliverable

Brick paths over continuous lawn, a main cross, paired panels of crossing diagonals, triangular lawns, perimeter walks, blossom framing, off-route seating and destination connections. Include overhead/player-height evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- The main cross and paired crossed diagonal panels read clearly against the supplied overhead photo.
- Brick paths overlay a continuous blossom landscape; lawns remain visually connected rather than unrelated islands.
- Multiple approaches connect arrival, cottage, UDistrict, dog park and birthday courtyard.
- Blossom trunks/canopies, benches and planting leave primary routes and normal camera sightlines usable.
- Reading/gathering benches sit off circulation and face usable space.
- Materials, petals, flowers and landscape detail replace bare temporary masses while retaining the soft warm style.
- Authored colors and actual imported color textures satisfy the approved palette gate, with the explicit scoped community-tree exception below.
- Low-graphics and group-photo views retain recognizable path and blossom composition.

## Limits and open decisions

This is a UW-inspired birthday neighborhood, not a survey. Orientation and scale may adapt while preserving reference topology. Campus interiors, compulsory NPC Lisa and new minigame implementation are outside this revision. Human visual acceptance is pending.

## Validation

Compare overhead and ground views with image-1.png; traverse axes, diagonals and destination links. Inspect canopy obstruction, benches, collision, low graphics and group framing. Bind source, live palette capture and current place through MAP-02.
