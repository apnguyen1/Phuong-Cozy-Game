**Status:** Planned; not started  
**Priority:** P2 — after foundation  
**Suggested owner:** Environment art (unassigned)  
**Dependencies:** 01; 03  
**Owned scope:** Future bedroom shell and props; not quest logic

## Purpose

Make the one interior immediately recognizable as her room.

## Deliverable

Sage bed, round-bellied dog/dinosaur plushies, ivy/light curtain, white desk/shelves, wood floor, window, closet recess, TV, botanical print and mirror.

## Acceptance criteria

- Bed/light wall opposes desk/shelf wall.
- Exactly one detailed room.
- Entry and station clearances retained.
- Personal props recognizable on low settings.
- 4–6 active visitors can move around stations.

## Limits and open decisions

60 × 56 is enlarged game space, not a measured room. No extra library/living/craft rooms; missing plush details remain pending.

## Validation

Compare views with both room photos; later orbit and pass-through rehearsal.

Ticket creation is authorized. Implementation, scripting, Studio building and publishing are not started in this task. The user explicitly requested no code.
