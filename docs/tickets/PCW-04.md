# PCW-04 — Recreate Phuong's bedroom

**Status:** Implementation authorized; Draft 02 under independent review. Human acceptance is pending.

**September 26, 2026 scope amendment:** The active September 26 request authorizes one photographed bedroom inside the existing cottage. This supersedes the earlier no-code boundary and provisional room dimensions, not the recognizable features or clearance requirements. Historical text is preserved below.

## Purpose

Make the cottage interior recognizable as Phuong's photographed bedroom with comfortable multiplayer circulation.

## Deliverable

One detailed bedroom in the existing cottage: sage layered bedding, dog/dinosaur plushies, ivy/light curtain, white desk and filled shelves, wood floor, window dressing, closet recess, TV, botanical print, mirror and photographed small furnishings. Persisted builder source and actual Studio evidence accompany it.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exactly one detailed room occupies the existing cottage; the original exterior entrance remains usable.
- The bed/light wall opposes the desk/shelf wall, as in the two supplied room photographs.
- Sage bedding, folded blankets, personal plushies, ivy, lights, white desk/shelves, wood floor, closet, TV, botanical print and mirror remain recognizable from normal player views.
- Entry, front-door swing and routes to the bed, desk, shelves and closet remain clear; the third-person camera works under the ceiling.
- Personal props remain recognizable on low graphics settings.
- A rehearsal with 4–6 active visitors demonstrates usable circulation around the stations.
- Authored colors use the approved palette; any imported color texture has its actual verified source.
- The personal birthday note remains entirely user-authored and is not generated as decoration.

## Limits and open decisions

The established enlarged game cottage is retained; this is a stylized reconstruction, not a measured copy. The shallow wardrobe is part of this room. No extra library/living/craft room, quest, exact branded shelf artwork or invented personal message is added. Human acceptance is pending.

## Validation

Compare actual Studio views against both supplied room photographs. Walk the entrance and stations, check door/camera, review low graphics, rehearse 4–6 visitors, and inspect console/palette evidence. MAP-02 binds the integrated submission.
